The Artisan Behind The Blade

Elevating Personal Style Through Precision Texture & Form

Founded by Master Barber Alex Coutinho, The Obsidian Edge was established on a simple philosophy: cutting hair is an architectural discipline tailored specifically to your head shape, hair density, and daily lifestyle.

Alex has established a sterling reputation across Union County and Northern New Jersey for diagnosing unruly hair types—from pin-straight Asian hair needing texture separation, to curly perms and surgical low taper fades.

✓ Texture Diagnostics

Cranial mapping and density assessment prior to cutting.

✓ Japanese Shear Artistry

Point-cutting and debulking without causing surface frizz.

✓ Modern Texture Perms

Effortless wave structure formulated for low-maintenance styling.

✓ Razor Sharp Detailing

Hot botanical steam towels and surgical straight-razor lines.
